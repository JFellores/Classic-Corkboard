# Changelog

## 0.7.3 — Add slides, checklists, and table cards

- Added numbered slides with no scrollbars on the cork surface. After each slide settles, its cards pop in and threads draw from their source pins. Right-click the cork to add a note, image, or table. Table cards support range selection, copy and paste, editable rows and columns, and five aggregate functions. Click or drag across cells while writing a formula to insert their reference. Select note text to turn it into a checklist item, change its size, or turn it into a clickable web link. Choose a daily checklist reset time on any note. Press Ctrl+Alt+B to minimize or restore the corkboard from any app.

## 0.6.0 — Move pinned notes and identify thread connections

- Moved the compact Board menu above the notes and added hover and keyboard focus to reveal its label. Menu backgrounds match the note paper or the selected thread. Threads show the labels of connected cards. Hold and drag a card’s pin to move it with its threads attached. Fixed the detached Corkboards submenu in the Windows tray.

## 0.5.2 — Postage-stamp frames

- Made smaller image cards look like perforated stamps and larger cards look like instant photos, with the same saved pin and thread connections.

## 0.5.1 — Instant-photo frames

- Gave image cards cream paper borders, bottom captions, and centered pins.

## 0.5.0 — Image cards

- Added local image imports, resizing and pop-outs for images, and support for pinning and connecting image cards.

## 0.4.2 — Pointer-following threads and pin colors

- Previewed threads under the pointer, allowed attachment by clicking a destination card, added independent pin colors, and moved hints below the notes.

## 0.4.1 — Rounded menus and titleless dialogs

- Restyled popup menus and label dialogs, moved the floating note menu into a footer, and cleared stale corner grip marks on resize.

## 0.4.0 — Cork boundaries and deletion

- Kept notes within the visible board, refreshed the floating Board menu, and added confirmed deletion for boards and notes.

## 0.3.1 — Thread controls and redraws

- Added thread colors to the pin menu and repainted the area exposed when a note moved or resized.

## 0.3.0 — Resizable notes and clearer controls

- Added mouse and keyboard resizing for notes, remembered dimensions, and refreshed command spacing and keyboard focus.

## 0.2.0 — Wooden frames and separate boards

- Added resizable wooden frames, independent corkboards, a saved-notes drawer, lockable pins, colored threads, labels, and rich text.

## 0.1.1 — Reliable tray minimize

- Verified Windows tray registration before hiding the board and kept the taskbar button available when registration failed.

## 0.1.0 — First corkboard

- Added a draggable corkboard, local autosave, pop-out notes, a tray icon, and an optional Windows startup setting.
